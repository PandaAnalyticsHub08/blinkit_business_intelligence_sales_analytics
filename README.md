🛒 Blinkit Business Intelligence & Sales Performance Analytics

An End-to-End Business Intelligence Project Using Power BI, SQL, DAX, Power Query & Excel

📌 Project Overview

The Blinkit Business Intelligence & Sales Performance Analytics project focuses on analysing business performance through an interactive six-page Power BI dashboard.

The project explores sales performance, product trends, customer behaviour, revenue growth, delivery operations, inventory management and marketing effectiveness to generate meaningful business insights and support data-driven decision-making.

🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyse overall sales and revenue performance.
- Identify top-performing products and categories.
- Understand customer purchasing behaviour.
- Track revenue growth and business trends.
- Evaluate delivery and inventory performance.
- Analyse marketing and offer effectiveness.
- Present key business insights through interactive dashboards.

 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

| Tool | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Power BI | Interactive dashboard development |
| SQL | Data querying and analysis |
| DAX | KPI calculations and analytical measures |
| Power Query | Data cleaning and transformation |
| Microsoft Excel | Dataset preparation and validation |

 📊 Dashboard Pages

 1. Executive Overview
Provides a high-level summary of business performance through key performance indicators and sales insights.

 2. Sales & Product Analytics
Analyses sales performance, product contribution and category-level trends.

 3. Customer Insights
Explores customer behaviour, purchasing patterns and customer-related performance.

 4. Advanced Revenue & Growth Analytics
Examines revenue trends, growth performance and time-based business metrics.

 5. Operations & Marketing
Evaluates delivery performance, inventory operations and marketing effectiveness.

6. Business Efficiency & Contribution Analytics
Highlights business efficiency, operational performance and contribution analysis.
